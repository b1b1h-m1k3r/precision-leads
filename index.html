01 / The real reason replies are low

One Message Can't Win Four Different Mindsets

Most outbound sends one email to the whole list, as if every buyer on it cares about the same thing. A plant manager is measured on uptime, a procurement lead on savings, a managing director on margin. A message written for all of them lands with none of them.

So we never run one campaign. We split your list by role, write each group its own email, and open it with a signal from their own company. Same list, same effort, about ten times the replies.
