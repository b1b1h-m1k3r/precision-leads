Questions people ask first

Is this right for my business?

It works best when you sell to other businesses, one new customer is worth more than about $5,000 to you, and you can describe who buys from you. It is usually not a fit for consumer products or very small deals. The free consultation is there to answer exactly this.

Will this hurt our brand or our email domain?

No. We send from separate domains that look like your brand, set up and warmed before any campaign starts. Your main company domain and your own inboxes are never touched, and the new domains belong to you.

Isn't cold email just spam?

Spam is the same message blasted to anyone. We write to a chosen group of decision makers, open every email with something specific about their company, and keep it short and easy to say no to.

What does the $2,500 setup cover?

Everything needed to launch and the first 60 days of running it: your list of companies and decision makers, verified emails, the copy, the sending domains and inboxes, and the software we use to send emails on your behalf.

What counts as a booked meeting?

A meeting counts when a decision maker who fits the plan we agreed on books a time on your calendar through our outreach.

How much of my time does this take?

About 2 hours in total: a 45-minute kickoff call, 20 minutes to approve the emails, then two 30-minute meetings every 4 weeks so you can tell us which leads are buying, so we can tweak the campaign and target more people with the same profile.
