About

I build the software sales teams use to clean, enrich and qualify their data. Precision Leads is me running that same process for you.

I co-founded Datablist and lead its growth team today. More than 37,000 people use it, including teams at Uber Freight, AWS and Airbus.

Before the software, I ran a lead generation agency. Cold email, cold calling, the usual. The pitch was rarely the problem. The data was. Lists went stale between building and sending. Contacts bounced. A campaign nobody was watching could burn through a market in a week, and you would only find out when the replies never came.

So I built what I was missing. Datablist cleans, enriches and qualifies B2B data, and it turned out a lot of other people had the same problem: it now runs inside sales and go-to-market teams that do this work in-house.

Most teams don't need another tool. They need someone to actually run the campaign, with the same discipline behind the data.

That is what this is. I take the process I built the software for and run it end to end: the brief, the list, the verification, the audience split, the copy, the sending, and every reply until a real buyer is on the calendar.

We stay deliberately small. A lean team and a few clients at a time, because precision is the whole product here. The moment you run twenty accounts with a pod of juniors, the data gets sloppy, the copy gets generic, and you are back to the campaigns I left the agency world to get away from.

My week is split between both sides of the same problem: the tooling at Datablist, and the campaigns running on it here.
